# MarketMinds-EndToEnd
End-to-end marketing analytics project using Python, SQL, and Power BI to uncover campaign performance and customer insights

### 🔹 Project Overview

**Market Minds** is an end-to-end marketing analytics project analyzing customer purchase behavior across multiple campaigns.

The project demonstrates **data cleaning and EDA in Python**, **analytical querying in SQL**, and **business storytelling in Power BI**.

---

### 🔹 Tech Stack

- **Python**: Pandas, Matplotlib
- **SQL**: CTEs, Window Functions, Aggregations
- **BI**: Power BI (DAX, Key Influencers)

---
### 🔹 Data Sources

- **marketmindz_research_data.csv**
  - Raw marketing campaign and customer behavior dataset used for analysis.

- **marketmindz_research_data_dictionary.csv**
  - Data dictionary describing column definitions and variable meanings.
---

### 🔹 Business Questions

1. Which marketing campaign generated the highest revenue?
2. What customer segments contribute most to revenue?
3. How do income and household composition affect purchasing behavior?
4. Which campaigns drive high-value repeat purchases?

---

### 🔹 Key Insights

- Campaign 6 generated **~38% of total revenue**, outperforming all others.
- High-income households with children showed **significantly higher purchase frequency**.
- Revenue is concentrated in the top **20% of customers**, suggesting strong upsell potential.
